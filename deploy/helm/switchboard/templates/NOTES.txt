switchboard {{ include "switchboard.imageTag" . }} is starting at {{ include "switchboard.publicUrl" . }}.

1. Wait for the pod to be Ready{{ if .Values.meetings.enabled }} (2/2 with the bot: the first start pulls joinly's 3.7 GB image, so it can wait in ContainerCreating for a few minutes){{ end }}:

     kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get pod {{ include "switchboard.fullname" . }}-0 -w

2. A fresh broker has no admin. It prints the admin's one-time password in its log; it works once,
   for 60 minutes (a new line is printed every hour until someone uses it). Take the newest:

     kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs {{ include "switchboard.fullname" . }}-0{{ if .Values.meetings.enabled }} -c switchboard{{ end }} | grep "one-time password"

   Sign in at {{ include "switchboard.publicUrl" . }} as admin with it, give your name and email, and
   choose your own password or a passkey.

   the Service {{ include "switchboard.fullname" . }}, port 7419, with TLS ended in front of it, and keep a quiet
   request open for 16 minutes (docs/DEPLOY.md, "Kubernetes").

database on start. The volume is kept by helm uninstall: delete the claim yourself to remove the data.
