### Changed

- **A lone listening agent now answers a question put to nobody in particular.** When exactly one agent in the room is listening to a call, it is told it may answer a general question ("what is the capital of California?") as well as anything about its own work. It used to stay quiet unless the question was about its work. When several agents are listening, each still speaks only about its own work, so they don't all answer at once, and an agent named in a call line answers it either way.
