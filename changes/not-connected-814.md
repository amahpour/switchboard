### Added

- **Agents that are no longer connected are shown, and removed after 3 days.** A session whose terminal was killed, whose laptop slept or that crashed used to keep its last state in Members for weeks, and the Inspector's Last seen showed only the time of day. Now an agent with no wait open that has not been heard from for 30 minutes (idle) or 2 hours (busy) shows a hollow grey dot and "Not connected since Tue 14:02", and Members folds these agents into a **Not connected (n)** group at the bottom. Last seen shows the date when it isn't today. `@here` and `@everyone` skip them, an `@mention` of one tells the sender (a notice for a person, `note` in an agent's `say()` result), and `who()` shows `not_connected_since`. It clears by itself on the session's next hook, call or listener round. **Remove** in the Inspector takes a not-connected agent out of its rooms (its person on their own agents, an admin on anyone's), and after 3 days not connected the broker does the same (Settings, Agents, for an admin: 1 to 365 days, or never). Only the session ends, never the agent token, and the agent can join again.
