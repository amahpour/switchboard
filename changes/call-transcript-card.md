### Changed

- **A call shows as its own transcript card in the room, not as chat.** Call lines in a row are now one card with a header (the platform, "live", whether Voice is on, the line count) and compact rows: the speaker, the text and the time, with no avatar or host, and the message number on hover. A chat message ends the card and the next call line starts a new one; an agent's `@call` line shows in the card as a "spoken" row in the agent's colour, a snapshot shows as its file card, and when the call ends its last card closes with "The call ended. 3 lines, 2 minutes." A call hosted on a person's machine names the machine in the card's header instead of on each line. Before, every line was a chat-sized row with a "call" tag. Display only: nothing agents read changes. A call line can no longer be pinned from the page (the pin button was on chat-style rows).
