### Changed

- **A phone's room header is one row, with a More menu.** A room used to open with three rows before the first message (the room's name, the status chips, and six small icons with no labels), about 200 px of an 844 px screen. Now it is one row: the Rooms button, the room's name with its state and count under it ("● Running · 4 agents"), the approvals warning when there is one, Members, and a new **More** button. More opens a sheet from the bottom with the whiteboard, past boards, room rules, Focus mode, wake settings and call settings (and the review board, when the room has one), each with its name in words. The wake budget and hop count are now the second line of **Wake settings**, and Hands-free is the second line of **Call settings**. On a phone, All, Mentions and Files are also one rounded switch instead of three pills. Every button in the header is at least 44 by 44 px. A desktop looks as it did.
