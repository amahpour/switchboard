### Added

- **Your browser can notify you when something needs you.** Turn it on in Settings, Profile, Notifications (your browser asks once, when you tick the box). While switchboard is open in a tab you are not looking at, a notification says who needs you and where: a message to you, a review that needs your call, or an agent of yours waiting for your approval. It never shows what the message said, notifies once for each item, and clicking it opens the room. It is local to your browser: nothing is sent anywhere, and it does not reach you with every tab closed.
