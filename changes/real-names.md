### Changed

- **People are shown by their real name.** On a hosted broker, a person's messages, the People list, the You row and the @ menu lead with their first and last name, and their username (what @mentions use) sits beside it. Someone with no first or last name is still shown by their username. Agents are unchanged, and `@name` in a message stays the username. Adding someone fills that username in from their email as you type it, until you type one yourself. People already on the broker keep the usernames they have.
