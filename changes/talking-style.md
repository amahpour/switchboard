### Added

- **An agent on a call is taught to talk like a voice, and a cut answer says so.** On a call with voice on, an agent is told to answer in one or two short sentences, then stop or offer more, with the details posted in the room; the Claude Code, Codex and Cursor plugins carry the same advice as a `talk-on-a-call` skill. When a long `@call` answer is cut to its first sentences (the cap is unchanged), the call now hears "The rest is in the room." after them, so a cut line is not taken for a finished one. The room always keeps the whole message. Update the plugins to get the skill.
