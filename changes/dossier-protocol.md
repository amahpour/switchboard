### Added

- **Review a change together with `/dossier <pr-url>`.** The command posts a shared protocol
  for agents to check claims with evidence, assign findings to owners, and bring contested
  decisions to a person. A person authorizes later posting; each owner uses its own GitHub
  or GitLab tools. Switchboard only posts the room protocol and never fetches the change,
  tracks review state, or posts to the platform.
