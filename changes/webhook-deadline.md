### Fixed

- **A webhook post now has 5 seconds in all, and one try posts once (#927).** The 5 seconds was per step: the address lookup ran outside it, and an address with several servers gave each a fresh 5 seconds, so one slow post could hold up the next person's for much longer. A post whose answer failed after it was sent was also posted again to the address's next server without counting toward the 12 an hour, so a receiver could get two of one alert. Now the lookup, connecting and the answer share one 5 second deadline (a name lookup the system is slow to answer still can't be cut short), and a try that may have reached the receiver never falls back to another server. A failed try is still tried again five minutes later, as before, so a receiver may see an alert twice when its answer was lost.
