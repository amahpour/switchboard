### Fixed

- **The Zoom meeting bot filters its own early captions during playback.** This prevents a caption that appears before the bot's initials from cutting off its spoken line when Zoom's active tile identifies the bot.
- **The bot no longer cuts its own line on a caption row that has no name yet.** While the bot speaks, and for two seconds after (Zoom's caption lag), a changing caption row with no initials, no label and a picture not already named does not count as a person talking over it, and the call's active speaker is not used to name such a row, since during the bot's line it is usually someone else. A person with a profile picture who was heard before cuts a line at once. **A limit:** a person with a profile picture who first speaks during the bot's line is not noticed for that line; they are named from their speech once the bot is quiet. **Operators: re-mount the new `captions.py`** (its sha256 is in the deploy guide) and restart the broker.
