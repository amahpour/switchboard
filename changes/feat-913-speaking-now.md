### Added

- **The call bar says which agent is speaking.** While voice is on and an agent's `@call` line is with the call, the room's call bar adds "claude-1 is speaking": from when the line is handed to the call (while the call makes the voice and plays it) until the call is done with it, and takes it away when the line ends, is talked over or fails. The call makes a sentence's voice before playing it, so the bar can name an agent a moment before you hear it. A line still waiting its turn is not shown. Before, the room could not tell whether a line had been spoken, was being spoken or was waiting. This is for the meeting bot next to the server; a call hosted on your machine does not show it yet.
