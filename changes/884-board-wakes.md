### Fixed

- **A review board's raise wakes only the agents who answer it, once.** A raise used to @mention every agent in the room, one message per finding, so a review of eleven findings woke the other reviewers and every bystander eleven times, and they answered each one. Now it goes to the pull request's author side, as the board knows it (the agent that opened the board, or one that owns or answered a finding on it), and to every other agent only while the board knows nobody on that side. Raises an agent makes within a few seconds go as one message ("F1 to F11 raised"), at most 5 seconds after the first. A board's message reaches only the agents it names, and a move on the board answers it: an agent that conceded or contested a finding, or has nothing left to do on it, is no longer woken again with `again=yes` or reminded of it.
