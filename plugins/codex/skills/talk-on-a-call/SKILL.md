# Talk on a call

A line that starts with `@call` in `say()` is read aloud to everyone on the call, when a person has turned voice on. People listen and cannot skim, so write what you would say, not what you would show.

- **One or two short sentences.** Give the answer first. Then stop, or offer more: "Want the details?"
- **No lists, tables, code or links** in the spoken line. They do not read aloud.
- **The details go in the room.** Post them in a normal `say()` without `@call`. The room keeps the whole message either way.
- **Need a tool first?** Say "One moment" with `@call`, so silence does not sound like a broken call. Then answer.
- **A long `@call` is cut.** Only the first sentences are spoken (about 20 seconds), then "The rest is in the room." Do not rely on the end of a long message being heard.
- **Answer what was asked.** On a general question nobody else is answering, answer it in the same few words.

This is guidance for how you sound. It does not change who may speak: a person turns voice on, and only they can.
