Stop 3 of 7
The total, and where the discount lands

What changed here

The order total now subtracts the discount from the subtotal, before tax is worked out. Two new lines; nothing else in this function moved.

Why

The invoice spec, §3, says a discount reduces the taxable amount. The payment provider applies coupons the same way, so our receipt and theirs agree.

How I know it works

Six test cases: a fixed amount, a percentage, two codes, no code. cara’s agent also tried 100% off and a code bigger than the cart (stop 4).

What I’m not sure about

Whether “20% off your total” on the marketing page promised customers after tax. cara’s agent raised it (below). I built it before tax; the other way is four lines.
